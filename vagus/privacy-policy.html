Vagus

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 9, 2026

Overview

Vagus is a music release tracker and organizer. You follow artists, sort them with tiers and tags, and the app notifies you when they release new music. This policy explains what data the app collects, who it's shared with, and how long it's kept.

Vagus is developed and operated by a single developer. Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

What we collect

Data Why it's collected
Email address, and name and profile photo if you use Google To identify your account and sync your library across devices. Provided by Google Sign-In, or by you if you sign up with email and a password. Both go through Firebase Authentication, which stores your password securely; Vagus never sees it. If you sign up with email, Firebase sends you messages to verify your address or reset your password.
Artists you track Stored in Firestore to sync your library across devices and run the daily release check pipeline.
Tags and tiers Labels you assign to artists, stored in your account to organize and filter your library and to restore them if you reinstall.
Inbox and release history Releases added to your inbox (album name, release date, artist) and which ones you've marked. Stored per-user in Firestore.
Push notification token A device token issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging, used to deliver new release notifications.
Subscription status Purchase receipts and subscription state are managed by RevenueCat to verify access to premium features. Vagus does not store payment card details.
Last.fm credentials (optional) If you choose to connect Last.fm, your username (and your own API key, if you add one) is stored on your device only and is never sent to Vagus servers. It is used to read your listening history from Last.fm. That history stays on your device, where it marks what you've already heard.

Local cache

Vagus stores a local database on your device containing your artist list, release catalogue, and inbox so the app works without waiting on a network request each time. This mirrors your Firestore data and is cleared when you delete your account, when a different account signs in on the device, or when you uninstall the app.

What we don't collect

  • Your Spotify account data, listening history, or playlists
  • Listening or playback data on our servers (Last.fm history stays on your device)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Microphone or camera access
  • Any app data from users who are not signed in

Third-party services

Vagus uses the following third-party services to function. Each service's privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Firebase (Google)

Used for account authentication (Firebase Auth), cloud data storage (Firestore), push notifications (Cloud Messaging), and server-side processing (Cloud Functions). Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Spotify Web API

Used to search for artists and retrieve artist/album metadata using an app-level access token. Vagus does not connect to your Spotify account and cannot access your Spotify library, listening history, playlists, or playback. No user Spotify credentials or tokens are collected or stored. Governed by Spotify's Privacy Policy.

Deezer

Used to fetch release catalogue and artist metadata via Deezer's public API. No user account or authentication is required. Governed by Deezer's Privacy Policy.

Apple iTunes Search

Used to improve artist search and genres. When you search for an artist, your phone sends the search text and your country to Apple. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Wikidata and Wikipedia

Our servers use these to look up artist information. No user data is sent.

Exportify and TuneMyMusic (optional)

If you choose to import a playlist or send music to a playlist, Vagus directs you to these services and you use them directly. Vagus never receives your sign-in details for them. Their own privacy policies apply.

MusicBrainz

Used to look up track listings for a small number of older releases. No user data is sent. Governed by the MetaBrainz Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

Used to manage in-app subscriptions and verify purchase receipts. RevenueCat may receive your device's purchase receipt, a device identifier, and your Vagus account ID. Governed by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Last.fm (optional)

If you choose to enable Last.fm integration by supplying your username and API key, Vagus reads your listening history from Last.fm to show which tracks you've heard. Your Last.fm credentials are stored only on your device. Governed by Last.fm's Privacy Policy.

Suggestion form (Google Forms)

The "Suggest a feature" form on our website is separate from the app and is hosted by Google Forms. It collects your suggestion and, only if you choose to give it, your email address, which is used only to reply to you. To have a submission deleted, contact us at the address below. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention & deletion

Your Firestore data (artists, inbox, tags) is retained as long as your account exists. Your local device cache is cleared when you delete your account, when a different account signs in on the device, or when you uninstall the app.

To delete your account and its data, go to Settings > Delete account in the app, which takes effect right away. If you can't open the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page and we will complete your request within 30 days. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel that in Google Play.

Children

Vagus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
