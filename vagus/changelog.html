Release notes

What changed in each version of Vagus, newest first.

Version 1.0.4

November 24, 2026
  • A fresh teal look that matches the Vagus icon.
  • History reaches further back, even when most of your recent plays were videos.
  • YouTube videos no longer show as Now Playing in History.
  • Unmarking a release keeps it unmarked, even if Last.fm says you've played it.

Version 1.0.3

November 13, 2026
  • Suggest a feature straight from Settings.
  • Filters now open from the bottom of the screen and show every option at once.
  • Settings groups genres the same way the filters do.
  • Fix an artist's tags right from a release in the Inbox or Catalogue.
  • The Back button now leaves multi-select.
  • Catalogue no longer jumps to the top when you start selecting.
  • Fixed new releases sometimes missing from the Inbox until you reopened the app.
  • Imported artists get their releases sooner.
  • Stop tracking an artist from their tag sheet, wherever you open it.
  • The artist page no longer jumps when you start selecting releases.
  • Multi-select shows Mark as listened or Unmark only when it would change something.
  • Catalogue collaborations name the other artists you track, like "with GIVĒON".
  • Switching tabs ends multi-select.
  • A newly added artist shows their releases right away.
  • Artist search hides empty duplicate profiles, so you see one Kamasi Washington, not three.
  • Vagus now speaks Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Indonesian, following your phone's language.
  • Inbox and Catalogue share the album, EP, single and listened filters.
  • A scroll bar appears while you scroll your artists. Drag it to jump through the list.
  • Marking a release listened in the Catalogue or on an artist page marks it in the Inbox too, and the other way round.
  • When a new version is out, Vagus offers to update without leaving the app.

Version 1.0.2

November 10, 2026
  • Fixed tiers and tags sometimes going missing after reinstalling Vagus.
  • If Google sign-in fails, Vagus now says so instead of doing nothing.
  • Rate Vagus from Settings.
  • The tier, tag and genre picker now has a Done button. Swiping it away still saves too.
  • Sign in with email and a password if you would rather not use Google.
  • Your Last.fm username now comes back when you sign in again or reinstall.
  • New release notifications now stay together in one group.
  • After an update, a short note tells you what changed. You can turn it off in Settings.

Version 1.0.1

October 31, 2026
  • The intro now shows how to send music to a playlist.
  • Fixed Google sign-in failing for people who installed Vagus from Google Play.

Version 1.0.0

October 29, 2026
  • First release on Google Play. Thanks for testing!
  • Filter Artists, Inbox and Catalogue by tier, tag, genre and release type. Tap a filter twice to exclude it.
  • Genres are grouped into families like Rock, Jazz and Hip-Hop.
  • Artist search shows artists you follow, listen to, or that sound like them first, and says why.
  • Swipe the Artists list sideways to switch tiers.
  • Make a playlist from the Inbox, the Catalogue or any artist's page. The playlist file includes each song's exact recording code, so songs with common titles land on the right version.
  • Choose which tiers and tags send you notifications.
  • Import artists from Apple Music and other playlists through TuneMyMusic.
  • New release notifications show album art.
  • Artists that showed a blank picture now get their photo from Spotify.
  • Delete your account from Settings.
