How it works

Every new release, in one Inbox

New albums, EPs and singles from the artists you follow land here, newest first. What you've already heard fades out, so the new music stands out.

Rank your artists your way

Sort them into tiers, from S for the ones you never want to miss, and add tags of your own like "Check out" or "Gym". Genres are added for you.

Find exactly what you want to hear

Filter by tier, tag, genre or release type. Tap a filter twice to hide it instead, for "everything except Gym".

Send it all to a playlist

Select everything you haven't heard in one tap, then send it to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music or another app.

Every album and single in one place

Each artist's full discography, from their newest single back to their first album, with the songs you've played checked off.

See what you've been playing

Connect Last.fm and your plays show up here, matched to your artists. Add anyone you've been listening to with one tap.

Why Vagus exists
Find music I like easily. Then listen to it easily.

I made Vagus for a problem I had.

I love music, but finding new music from the artists I like was harder than it should be. There was no one place to see it all. Following artists on Spotify felt clunky, and nothing really told me when they released something.

So I made the app I wanted. Vagus tells me when my artists release new music, and lets me sort those artists my way. When I want to hear something specific, I pick a tier or tag, see exactly what fits, send it to a playlist and listen. That simple.

Vagus is Latin for wanderer. Inspired by the vagus nerve.

FAQ

Is Vagus free?

Yes. Vagus is free right now, with no ads.

Which music apps does it work with?

Any of them. Vagus doesn't play music itself: tap a release to open it in your streaming app, or send a whole selection to a playlist in Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and others through TuneMyMusic.

How do I add my artists?

Search for them by name, or import a whole playlist at once: export it as a CSV file (from Spotify with exportify.net, or from other apps with TuneMyMusic) and pick the file in Vagus.

What are tiers and tags?

Tiers rank your artists: S for the ones you never want to miss, then A, B and so on. Tags are your own labels, like "Check out" or "Gym". Filter the Inbox by any of them to see just the new music you're after.

How does Vagus know what I've listened to?

Connect Last.fm and Vagus marks releases as listened when they show up in your listening history. You can also mark releases yourself.

How quickly does new music show up?

Vagus checks for new releases every morning, with extra checks early on Friday when most music comes out, so Friday releases usually appear within a few hours.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. Vagus is on Android for now.
