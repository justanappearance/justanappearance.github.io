How it works

Your artists, in order. Their new music, in one place.

Follow the artists you care about and Vagus checks every one of them for you.

Organize

Rank artists into tiers, add your own tags, and get genres added for you.

Stay on top

Notifications for the tiers and tags you choose.

Find

Filter new releases by tier, tag, genre or type, so you only see what you're after.

Listen

Select everything you haven't heard and send it to Spotify, Apple Music or another app.

Explore

Browse every artist's full discography in the Catalogue.

Why Vagus exists
I followed many artists, had no good way to organize them, and kept missing new releases.

From the developer

Streaming apps are built for playing music, not for keeping track of the people who make it. New albums get buried, and a long list of followed artists turns into a wall of names.

Vagus is the app I wanted: one Inbox for everything new, and tiers and tags so the artists I care about most are always easy to find.

Vagus is Latin for wandering. It's for wandering through music, and finding your way back to the artists you love.

Shaped by the people using it.

Testers asked for email sign-in and a Done button on the pickers. Both shipped within days. See what people asked for, and what changed.
