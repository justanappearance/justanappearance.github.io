Vagus

Find the music you love.

Organize the artists you love exactly the way you think about them, with tiers and tags of your own, and finding the music you want to hear is easy.

Now in testing on Google Play
How it works
  • Organize: rank artists into tiers, add your own tags, and get genres added for you
  • Find: filter new releases by tier, tag, genre or type, so you only see what you're after
  • Listen: select everything you haven't heard and send it to Spotify, Apple Music or another app
  • Explore: browse every artist's full discography in the Catalogue
  • Stay on top: notifications for the tiers and tags you choose
